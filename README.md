# Seasonal World

Seasonal World brings the seasons to Kanto in **Pokémon Red, Blue and Yellow for Gen1Recomp**. All 42 outdoor maps change with your device’s local date, including all 25 numbered routes, Viridian Forest, Vermilion Dock and the four outdoor Safari Zone areas.

**[Download Seasonal World 1.4.0-alpha.14 for Gen1Recomp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tooandorisu/Seasonal-World/main/downloads/seasonal_world-1.4.0-alpha.14.zip)**

## Installation

1. Import the mod ZIP linked above in Gen1Recomp. For a manual install, extract the `seasonal_world` folder into `mods`. When updating, keep one installed copy.
2. Enable **Seasonal World - Late Winter Preview**, then close Gen1Recomp completely and reopen it.
3. Leave **SEASON** on **AUTO (DATE)** to follow your local calendar, or choose a test mode to visit a season immediately.

## The year in Kanto

| Season | Local dates in AUTO | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spring | March 1–May 31 | Follow drifting petals from the flower beds in Pallet Town to the trees in Viridian Forest. |
| Summer | June 1–August 31 | Dragonflies skim the Safari Zone ponds. In the evening, fireflies rise from the ferns on Route 1. |
| Autumn | September 1–November 30 | Ride through leaf piles on Cycling Road, or approach a rustling pile in Fuchsia City to spot Paras. |
| Christmas | December 1–January 6 | Snow covers roofs and paths. Face a decorated tree and press **A** for a greeting. |
| Late winter | January 7–the last day of February | Snow begins to break up on February 1. From February 20, small patches remain in sheltered spots. |

**New Year in Vermilion City.** Watch from the city pier between 23:30 on December 31 and 00:30 on January 1. Three bursts mark midnight, and confetti stays through January 1. Choose **NEW YEAR TEST** to see the finale at another time.

## Try the thaw

Stand just outside the Pokémon League doors at Indigo Plateau. With **SNOW COVER** enabled, switch from **LATE WINTER TEST** to **THAW TEST**. The snowy approach gives way to small patches at the foot of the stone wall. At Vermilion Dock, try the same comparison on the quay beside the gangway.

The **SEASON** menu also includes **SPRING TEST**, **SUMMER TEST**, **AUTUMN TEST**, **CHRISTMAS TEST** and **OFF**. Adjust **SNOWFALL**, **SNOW COVER**, **FALLING PETALS**, **SUMMER MOTION** and **AUTUMN MOTION** separately.

## Compatibility and feedback

Use the standard flat 2D overworld view to see the seasonal effects. TileShift is optional. Alternate tilesets can shift the alignment of decorations. When reporting an issue, include the map or crossing, game edition, season setting, tileset mods and a screenshot if possible.

The mod ZIP includes a detailed guide with more places to visit in each season. [View the changelog](CHANGELOG.md).
